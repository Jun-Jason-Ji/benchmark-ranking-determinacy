One paragraph. When a simulator is calibrated from real demonstration trajectories, the SIMPLER-style free-space replay protocol identifies only the stiffness/damping ratio of the joint PD controller and the execution delay; the common stiffness/damping scale, the torque limit, and object friction/density are exactly invisible to the calibration data. Varying these calibration-invisible parameters over a reasonable range never produced a CI-supported flip of the ranking sign between Octo-small and Octo-base on four WidowX tasks, but it did change whether a ranking can be declared: point calibration (nominal parameters only) declares small better on the spoon and eggplant tasks, whereas taking bounds over the whole compatible parameter set forces abstention on all three informative tasks. Replacing per-condition extrema by a response surface with a simultaneous confidence band restores the spoon conclusion but keeps eggplant undecided. A stronger interim finding ("object friction changes decidability on eggplant") failed to replicate under a second seed set, and we found that rollouts with identical seeds diverge after the inference server restarts (GPU numerical nondeterminism). All single-seed-set "decidable / flip" statements are therefore flagged as pending replication. With a second model family, the first instance that holds under two seed sets appeared: torque limit ×0.5 (exactly invisible to calibration) doubles OpenVLA-7B's eggplant success rate, and Octo-small's decidable advantage over OpenVLA falls to abstain in both seed sets (section 5.8).

1. 研究问题与核心命题1. Research question and thesis

令联合未知量 z = (θ, ν, κ)（动力学、观测标定、控制接口参数），D 为真实校准证据。固定任务与初始状态分布，对同一 z 比较两个策略：Δij(z) = Jsim(πi; z) − Jsim(πj; z)。问题是：校准数据提供的哪些信息足以支持策略比较？哪些额外证据最有效地消除与策略选择有关的歧义？

Let z = (θ, ν, κ) be the joint unknown (dynamics, observation calibration, control-interface parameters) and D the real calibration evidence. For a fixed task and initial-state distribution, two policies are compared at the same z: Δij(z) = Jsim(πi; z) − Jsim(πj; z). The question: which information in the calibration data suffices to support a policy comparison, and which additional evidence most efficiently removes the ambiguity that matters for the choice?

命题。完整参数辨识不是策略差值辨识的必要条件；有限数据下的整体轨迹拟合精度也不是排序可靠性的充分指标。决定因素是未确定方向对策略差值的影响，以及策略差值相对实践阈值 δ 的间隔。局部形式：若参数置信集合为椭球、差值可线性化为 Δ̂ + gᵀ(z − ẑ)，则半宽 h = q·√(gᵀ ID−1 g)，实践优势的充分判据是 |Δ̂| > δ + h + η（η 为仿真采样、代理和结构偏差的总半宽）。

Thesis. Full parameter identifiability is not necessary for identifying the policy difference, and the overall trajectory-fit accuracy under finite data is not a sufficient indicator of ranking reliability. What matters is how the undetermined directions move the policy difference, and the margin of that difference over a practical threshold δ. Locally: if the parameter confidence set is an ellipsoid and the difference linearises as Δ̂ + gᵀ(z − ẑ), the half-width is h = q·√(gᵀ ID−1 g) and a sufficient criterion for a practical advantage is |Δ̂| > δ + h + η, with η the total half-width from simulation sampling, surrogate and structural error.

三个必须分开的对象：结构可辨识性（无限同类观测能否区分）、有限样本证据强度（当前区间多宽）、实践优势（真实差值是否超过 δ）。

Three objects kept separate throughout: structural identifiability (can infinitely many observations of this type distinguish?), finite-sample evidence strength (how wide is the current interval?), and practical advantage (does the true difference exceed δ?).

4.2 校准回放（B-1，SIMPLER sysid 协议移植）4.2 Calibration replay (B-1, ported SIMPLER sysid protocol)

  1. 读取 BridgeData V2 示范（状态、动作、夹爪），CRC 校验。
  2. Read BridgeData V2 demos (states, actions, gripper) with CRC checks.
  3. 在 Empty-v1 场景中把机器人抬高 1 m，从固定的规范关节位形用 IK 对齐首帧末端位姿（初始误差 0.1 mm，2π 回绕处理满转关节）。
  4. In the Empty-v1 scene, lift the robot 1 m and align the first end-effector pose by IK from a fixed canonical joint configuration (initial error 0.1 mm; 2π wrapping for full-turn joints).
  5. 开环回放示范动作序列，逐步记录仿真末端位姿；误差 = 平移 L2 + 旋转 arcsin(‖Rpred − Rgt‖F / 2√2)。
  6. Replay the demo actions open-loop, logging the simulated end-effector pose each step; error = translation L2 + rotation arcsin(‖Rpred − Rgt‖F / 2√2).
  7. 对 sweep_v1 与 5×5×2 网格（刚度 × 阻尼 ∈ {0.5, 0.7, 1, 1.4, 2}，时延 ∈ {0, 1}）重复；用 check_iso_invariance.py 检查等比组内轨迹差（容差 1 mm）。
  8. Repeat for sweep_v1 and a 5×5×2 grid (stiffness × damping ∈ {0.5, 0.7, 1, 1.4, 2}, delay ∈ {0, 1}); check within-iso-ratio-group trajectory deviations with check_iso_invariance.py (tolerance 1 mm).
  9. 相容集合规则：条件 c 相容 ⇔ 配对自助的 mean(errc − errnominal) 单侧 95% 下界 ≤ 0。
  10. Compatible-set rule: condition c is compatible ⇔ the one-sided 95% lower bound of the paired-bootstrap mean(errc − errnominal) is ≤ 0.

4.3 统计与报告规则4.3 Statistical and reporting rules

  • 单率：Wilson 95%；差值：按 episode_id 配对自助 10 000–20 000 次。
  • Single rates: Wilson 95%; differences: paired bootstrap by episode_id, 10 000–20 000 resamples.
  • 集合判定（并集界）：L = min 集合内各条件 CI 下界，U = max 上界；L > 0 或 U < 0 才下结论，否则拒判。
  • Set verdict (union bound): L = min over compatible conditions of the CI lower bound, U = max of upper bounds; declare only if L > 0 or U < 0, otherwise abstain.
  • 响应面判定：GP 同时 95% 带的带内极值；配对平坦性检验拒绝平坦的轴不使用响应面，回退到并集界。
  • Response-surface verdict: extremes of the GP's simultaneous 95% band over the strip; any axis where the paired flatness test rejects flatness falls back to the union bound.
  • 任何"可判定 / 翻转"主张必须在 ≥ 2 个独立种子集下成立（2026-09-18 23:20 起）。
  • Any "decidable / flip" claim must hold under ≥ 2 independent seed sets (rule adopted 2026-09-18 23:20).
