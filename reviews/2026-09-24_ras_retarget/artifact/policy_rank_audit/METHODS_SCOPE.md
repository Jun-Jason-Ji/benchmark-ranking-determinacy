We provide a locally installable analysis component for repeated evaluations over a declared finite configuration inventory. Its input manifest records the implementation build, estimand, expected conditions and configurations, and the definition of an independent evaluation run. The component rejects incomplete inventories, computes equally weighted configuration means, and estimates uncertainty across complete independent census runs. It supports a normal working-model directional intersection--union test followed by Holm adjustment over the declared policy-pair family. Fewer than two runs, unsupported independence assumptions or zero empirical variance produce an unsupported abstention rather than a confidence interval. A separate utility computes continuous cost-weighted Neyman allocations for a fixed weighted-mean estimand and supplied pilot variances. This artifact does not provide automatic benchmark adapters, finite-sample coverage guarantees, an optimal general robot-evaluation design, or a bound on sim-to-real bias.
