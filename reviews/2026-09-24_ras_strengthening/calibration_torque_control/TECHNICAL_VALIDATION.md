# Separate half-torque control: technical validation

This technical check was completed before the secondary study's policy collection and inspected no policy success outcomes. It leaves every V3/reset-validation source and record unchanged.

The independent `fresh_reset_torque.py` adapter creates a new spoon environment for each configuration and completes canonical nominal settling. It then leaves nominal arm stiffness and damping unchanged and either retains the nominal force limits or halves the six arm force limits. No physics step is taken between settling and the first policy observation. Both conditions have zero external action delay. Force limits are assigned to the controller configuration, applied to the actual SAPIEN joints, and independently read back from both representations. Gripper drives, object masses, and collision-material properties are checked for equality before and after assignment.

All 48 tests passed: 24 configurations under nominal torque and the same 24 under half torque. Every saved full initial physical state and every initial RGB image exactly matched the previously frozen V3 canonical state/image for that configuration. Controller resets preserved the changed physical force limits and did not change the initial state. Controller references belonged to the current scene; every environment closed with scene and agent references cleared. The adapter does not implement policy logic or request policy inference.

The separate runner `execute_cell.py` accepts only `--condition forcehalf`, the same two policy names and seed bases as the frozen secondary plan, and checks canonical state/image equality before any policy call. It checks nominal stiffness/damping, half force, and zero delay explicitly, then runs the fixed 60-step endpoint with an isolated reseeded policy session. The 60th control step must reach the registered truncation limit. Actual drives, controller configuration, gripper properties, object mass/material, and clean environment closure are checked again before a completed episode is appended. References are discarded between fresh environments.

Validation took 18.65 seconds in total. Mean initialization time was 0.211 seconds (median 0.190, maximum 1.195). These are initialization-only costs, not estimates of policy inference or full study runtime. Versions were ManiSkill 3.0.1, SAPIEN 3.0.3, PyTorch 2.7.1+cu128, Gymnasium 1.3.0 and NumPy 1.26.4 on the existing RTX 5060 Laptop GPU rendering device with CPU physics.

Evidence files are `FROZEN_TECHNICAL_PROTOCOL.json`, `torque_reset_records.jsonl`, and `RESET_VALIDATION_RESULTS.json`. The technical protocol records source hashes before the checks. The broader statistical and execution freeze is owned separately by `PLAN.md`/`FROZEN_PLAN.json` and must precede any policy collection. This validation supports controlled initialization and parameter assignment; it does not establish the effect of the perturbation, a torque-saturation mechanism, benchmark equivalence, or real-world performance.

The 384 additional half-torque rollouts will share the 384 nominal V3 baselines. They are not a second independent baseline and must not be counted twice. Combined with V3's 384 fitted-setting rollouts, the complete joint design contains 1,152 unique rollouts.
