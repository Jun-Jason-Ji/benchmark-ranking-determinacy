Should your policy be classified as open-source? We are loosely designating policies as open-source if all the robot data used to train the model is (or will be) publicly available, and if any pre-trained weights are used in the model, those are also publicly available. In other words your model is (or will eventually be) reproducible.

For example, pi0_fast_droid isn't open-source because Pi data isn't released, but paligemma_fast_droid is because the only robot data used is DROID, which is released.

The open-source designation will appear in the leaderboard.
