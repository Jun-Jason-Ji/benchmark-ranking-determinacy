The fitted-point fibre has 2 conditions where the nominal fibre has 6, and the asymmetry is deliberate. The common-scale invariance is verified in 14 of the replay grid's 26 (ratio, delay) groups, across seven ratios from 0.5 to 2.0, to within 7.2 um -- but ratio 0.25 has exactly one grid point per delay value, so at the fitted ratio the common scale is never varied and the invariance is inferred from the pattern rather than measured. We do not build on that inference. The halved torque limit does carry over, because the saturation argument concerns the commanded torque staying below the limit and is independent of the gain ratio. The middle row applies the same two-condition fibre at nominal so the set comparison is like-for-like.

## Reading

- Point verdicts **DISAGREE** across the two operating points.
- Matched set verdicts **DISAGREE**.
- The two settings differ in replay loss by 2.90 mm (5% bound 2.25 mm), which is why the calibration data reject nominal; they differ in $\Delta$ by +0.0313.

What this settles and what it does not. It settles whether \emph{this} ranking, on \emph{this} task, moves when the controller is set where the calibration evidence points instead of where the benchmark ships it. It does not establish that the two operating points agree in general, and it is one task and one policy pair.
