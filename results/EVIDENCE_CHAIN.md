# One pair end to end: `octo-small` vs `octo-base`, PutEggplantInBasketScene-v1

Scope, stated once and true of every row: all **64 configurations** enumerated (configuration sampling error exactly zero), **5 seed sets** (A', B, C, D, E), **one inference-stack build**, run observation unit, estimator Eq. (eq:var), empirical variance on both sides. Generated by `scripts/make_evidence_chain.py`.

The three quantities are not commensurable and the table exists to show that they are unrelated in magnitude. Column 2 is what the calibration protocol can see, in the composite units of Eq. (eq:loss). Column 4 is how far the policy difference moves, in probability points. A condition the protocol cannot distinguish from nominal at all moves $\hat\Delta$ as much as one it identifies.

## Reading it

- **Point calibration** evaluates the first row only: $\hat\Delta = +0.055$, [-0.008, +0.117], verdict *abstain*.
- **The union bound** takes the minimum lower bound and the maximum upper bound over the whole fibre: [-0.008, +0.244], verdict *abstain*.
- The two agree on this pair at this budget.

- The spread of $\hat\Delta$ across the fibre is +0.120, produced by conditions whose replay-loss differences from nominal are at most $3.1\times10^{-6}$ composite or are not measurable at all. That gap between what calibration resolves and what the policy comparison responds to is the chain this table closes.

## What this table does not do

- It does not carry the operating-point result. That needs a second census at the fitted controller setting and is Table 10 of the paper, on the same pair but at three seed sets so the two operating points are compared at a matched budget.
- It does not carry the torque-shift magnitude of Sect. 7.3. That effect is on the cross-family Octo-versus-OpenVLA pairs, where a large-displacement policy saturates contact torque; on this Octo-versus-Octo pair the same parameter moves $\hat\Delta$ by much less. Quoting the two together would be the splice this table is meant to prevent.
- It does not speak about the real robot. No row here has real-robot ground truth.
